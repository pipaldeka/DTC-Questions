TRENDS (20 Marks) Section A — Latest Drone Technology 1. The technology that enables a drone to analyse images and recognise objects automatically is called Artificial Intelligence. 2. A group of drones working together in a coordinated manner is known as a drone Swarm. 3. The technology that combines information from radar, cameras, and other sensors to improve detection is called Sensor fusion. 4. FPV stands for First Person View, a system that allows a pilot to see a drone's view through a camera. 5. A drone that uses a fibre-optic cable for communication instead of a conventional radio link is used to counter GPS jamming. 6. The technology that enables multiple drones to exchange information through interconnected communication links is known as a Mesh network. 7. A system designed to detect, track, identify, and counter unauthorised drones is called a Counter -UAS system. Section B — Operation Sindoor 8. Operation Sindoor was conducted by the Indian Armed Forces in May 2025. 9. One important lesson from Operation Sindoor was the growing importance of integrating unmanned aerial systems with Counter-drone technologies. 10. The process of collecting information about an area or activity using drones is known as Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance (ISR). 11. The use of decoy drones can complicate an opponent's Air defence system by creating additional detection and identification challenges. 12. The ability to detect and respond to incoming drones using multiple layers of defensive systems is known as Layered air defence. Section C — Russia–Ukraine War and Drone Warfare 13. The Russia–Ukraine war has demonstrated the growing importance of low-cost, mass-produced Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). 14. A drone designed to be used in a single mission and not recovered for reuse is commonly called a One-way attack drone. 15. The Russian long-range attack drone family commonly known as Geran has evolved from the Iranian-designed Shahed series. 16. The technology that allows a drone to navigate without relying entirely on satellite signals such as GPS is known as GPS denied navigation. 17. The technology that enables a drone to create a map of its surroundings while simultaneously determining its own position is known as Simultaneous Localisation and Mapping (SLAM). Section D — Future Trends and Counter-Drone Technology The technology that allows a drone to detect and avoid obstacles automatically during flight is known as Detect and Avoidance (DAA). 19. The use of electronic signals to disrupt or interfere with a drone's radio communications is commonly called electronic jamming. 20. The ability of different drone platforms and control systems to exchange information and work together is known as Inter-operability.
